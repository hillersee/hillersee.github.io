Sheet E-3 / Services

From Study Through Start-Up.

Our engineers bring over 140 years of collective experience in municipal utility electrical, instrumentation and control design and project management, on projects from $10,000 to over $50 million.

Power

Distribution, standby power, system studies, lightning protection and lighting.

  • Low, medium and high voltage power distribution systems
  • Emergency standby generator systems
  • Short-circuit, protective device coordination and arc flash studies
  • Lightning protection, grounding, bonding and surge protection
  • Lighting systems design

Instrumentation, Control & SCADA

Instrumentation, SCADA, telemetry, motor control and networking.

  • Process instrumentation and control systems
  • SCADA and operator interface systems
  • Telemetry systems, fiber optic and radio communication
  • Motor control systems, including VFD applications
  • Communications and networking systems
  • PLC and computer programming

Facilities

Treatment plants, pumping stations, fueling and seaport facilities.

  • Water and wastewater treatment plants
  • Stormwater pumping and control structures
  • Lift stations and ASR wells
  • Fueling systems, seaport storage and handling
  • Major fuel storage and refining facilities, U.S. and international

Project Services

From master planning and studies through construction administration and start-up.

  • Master planning and condition assessment studies
  • Constructability review and utility coordination
  • Energy audits, cost estimates and value engineering
  • Construction administration, inspection, testing and start-up
  • Security and fire alarm system design
