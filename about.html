Three Decades in Municipal & Utility Engineering.

Hillers Electrical Engineering, Inc. (HEE) has been in business in Boca Raton, Florida, since 1994.

Design, Control & Delivery

We offer electrical engineering design, control application programming and construction management. Our design work covers power, control, instrumentation and telemetry, along with PLC and computer programming and start-up assistance, for county and state municipal agencies and private industry.

The Methodology

We deliver projects on time and on budget. As a small firm, we offer personal involvement from key company principals, who take an active role in making sure all work meets your expectations and our own standards of quality.

In consulting, responsiveness is the key to a long-term relationship. We stay accessible and responsive to our clients, and our relationships with many municipalities and environmental consulting firms go back to 1994.
